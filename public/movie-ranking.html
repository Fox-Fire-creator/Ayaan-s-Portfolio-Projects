The best movies right now, according to Ayaan

Ayaan’s Top 4 Movies Right Now

Undercover Billionaire

Undercover Billionaire is a reality TV show that follows a successful entrepreneur as they go undercover in a new city with only $100 and a cell phone, trying to build a million-dollar business in 90 days. The show is inspiring and showcases the challenges and triumphs of entrepreneurship.

The Laundromat

The Laundromat is a drama film that follows a series of interconnected stories revealing the world of hidden money, corruption, and powerful elites. It shows how wealthy individuals use complex financial systems to hide their wealth and avoid laws. The movie is insightful and highlights the dark side of global finance, making it both educational and engaging.

SpiderMan: Brand New Day

SpiderMan: Brand New Day is a superhero film that follows Peter Parker as he navigates the challenges of being a hero while dealing with personal responsibilities. The movie is exciting and showcases the dynamic relationship between the hero and his loved ones.

Black Panther

Black Panther is a superhero film that follows T'Challa as he becomes the king of Wakanda and must protect his nation from external threats. The movie is visually stunning and explores themes of identity, heritage, and responsibility.
